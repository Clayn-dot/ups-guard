Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-11-14
  Changes:
    - First release for Factorio 2.1: a rewrite of the old UPS Guard as a standalone mod.
    - Four effect levels (minimal, medium, high, extreme) set a ready group of settings. Every part is also a separate setting, no server or planet names are built in.
    - Ground item and junk cleanup, map trimming, deleting planets nobody used, pausing and thinning far enemy bases, pollution and asteroid limits, expansion and pathfinding tuning, enemy count warnings.
    - One /upg command with player and admin subcommands, presets with a dry run, surface deletion and one chat card at the end of each pass.
